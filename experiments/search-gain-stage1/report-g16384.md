# Search-gain map aimed at 16384: greedy play against depth-3 search (cutoff 0)

Positions from 3,000 seeded greedy games of `checkpoints/ntuple/decision-8x6-tc-reset/games_2000000.npz`: mean score 314,913, 16384 in 75.2% of them. From each point's boards (the first time a game reached it) play goes on until the board holds 16384 or the game ends. Arrival value is the network's own estimate of the score still to come from the board it reached 16384 on: the shape it arrived in. Expected final score is the score at arrival plus that value, or the final score if the game ended first. Gains are search minus greedy on continuation 0, which share spawns until their moves differ, with 95% intervals.
